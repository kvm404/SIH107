# Products under compulsory registration: Scheme-II (CRS)

These products need registration with BIS under the Compulsory Registration Scheme (Scheme-II, self-declaration of conformity) and must carry the Standard Mark with a unique R-number. Each line gives the product category, the Indian Standard and the order.
