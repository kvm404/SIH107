# BIS standard marks and certification schemes at a glance

Hand-curated from BIS web pages. Wording follows the official pages.

## BIS standard marks
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/consumer-overview/for-consumers-faq/ -->
- ISI Mark and Eco Mark: for products covered under Scheme-I (product certification licence).
- Registration Mark: for products under the self-declaration of conformity scheme, Scheme-II (Compulsory Registration Scheme). Electronic products under CRS carry the Registration Mark with a unique R-number.
- Hallmark: for hallmarked gold and silver articles.

## Which scheme applies
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/the-bureau/about-bis/ -->
- Product Certification Scheme (Scheme-I, ISI mark): licence for Indian manufacturers after factory assessment and product testing.
- Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS, Scheme-II): registration based on self-declaration and a test report from a BIS recognised lab, for electronics and IT goods notified by MeitY, solar photovoltaic goods notified by MNRE and some chemicals.
- Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS): ISI mark licence for foreign manufacturers, for all products except electronics and IT goods notified by MeitY.
- Scheme-IV: Certificate of Conformity, for example stampings, laminations and cores of transformers.
- Hallmarking Scheme: purity marking of gold and silver jewellery and artefacts by BIS recognised Assaying and Hallmarking Centres, with registered jewellers.
- Management Systems Certification: IS/ISO 9001, 14001, 22000, 45001 and other management system standards.
- Laboratory Recognition Scheme: recognition of testing laboratories whose reports BIS accepts.

## Verifying a mark
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/bis-apps/ -->
The BIS Care app can verify an ISI marked product ("Verify Licence Details"), a hallmarked jewellery item by HUID ("Verify HUID") and an electronic product's R-number under CRS ("Verify R-Number under CRS"). Complaints about product quality or misuse of BIS marks can also be registered in the app.
