# BIS for students: Standards Clubs and Learning Science via Standards

Hand-curated from BIS pages. Wording follows the official pages.

## Standards Clubs
<!-- source: https://www.services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/BISBlog/bis-launches-learning-science-via-standards-series/ -->
BIS establishes Standards Clubs in educational institutions across India. Science teachers are trained to act as mentors for the clubs. Standards Clubs undertake student-centric activities like debates, quizzes and competitions, including standards-writing competitions. Students are taken on exposure visits to industries and laboratories to see manufacturing and testing processes, and to BIS offices developed as learning spaces. BIS provides financial support to the clubs for up to three activities in a year. BIS also runs online quizzes for students of Standards Clubs on quiz.bis.gov.in.

## Learning Science via Standards
<!-- source: https://www.services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/BISBlog/bis-launches-learning-science-via-standards-series/ -->
Learning Science via Standards is a BIS series of lesson plans that use scientific concepts, principles and laws to help students understand their practical use in manufacturing, functioning and testing of products, as stated in the relevant Indian Standards. The subjects relate to products used in daily life. BIS officials and resource persons deliver the lesson plans to students, and the lesson plans are hosted on the BIS website.

## Internships in BIS laboratories
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/laboratorys/laboratory-services-overview/laboratory-faq/ -->
BIS laboratories take interns under the BIS Laboratory Internship Scheme. Applications go to the concerned BIS laboratory; contact details are in the BIS laboratory directory at https://www.bis.gov.in/directory/laboratory/.
