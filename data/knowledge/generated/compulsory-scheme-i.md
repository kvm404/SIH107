# Products under compulsory BIS certification: Scheme-I (ISI Mark)

The Central Government makes BIS certification compulsory for these products through Quality Control Orders (QCOs). They must carry the ISI mark under a valid BIS licence. Each line gives the Indian Standard, the product and the Quality Control Order.
