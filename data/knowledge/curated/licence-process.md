# How to get a BIS licence for the ISI mark (Scheme-I)

Hand-curated from the BIS Guidelines for Grant of Licence (Central Marks Department-I, 25 February 2026) and the BIS product certification FAQ. Wording follows the official documents.

## Basis for grant of licence
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GrantofLicence-Guidelines-25Feb2026.pdf -->
The Bureau grants a licence based on successful assessment of the manufacturing infrastructure, production process, quality control and testing capabilities or arrangements of the manufacturer through a visit to its manufacturing premises. Conformity of the product to the relevant standard(s) is also established through third party laboratory testing, testing during the visit to the premises, or a combination of both. The applicant selects one of the two options for grant of licence (Option 1 or Option 2).

## Application
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GrantofLicence-Guidelines-25Feb2026.pdf -->
The application is made in Form-V as specified in BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations 2018, Scheme I. The applicant submits the documents required by Form-V and a self-evaluation cum verification report (Annexure-I of the guidelines). BIS accepts product certification applications online only, including all payments, on the Manakonline portal (www.manakonline.in). A separate application is needed for each product and Indian Standard, and for each factory location.

## Option 1: factory visit first, samples tested after
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GrantofLicence-Guidelines-25Feb2026.pdf -->
- The applicant applies with the documents listed for the application.
- BIS visits the factory to assess manufacturing infrastructure, production process, quality control and testing capabilities, and draws sample(s) for testing in third party laboratories.
- Samples may not be drawn when the licence is to be considered on the basis of factory testing only. Factory testing is permitted for products listed in Annexure-II(A) of the guidelines, for example bulky products or products that are difficult to transport.
- Products processed exclusively under Option 1 are listed in Annexure-II(B). All applications from foreign manufacturers are processed under Option 1.
- Applicants whose earlier licence for the same product at the same premises was cancelled, expired or surrendered due to non-conformity of samples can apply only under Option 1.

## Option 2: simplified procedure with third party test reports
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GrantofLicence-Guidelines-25Feb2026.pdf -->
- Products processed under Option 2 are listed in Annexure-II(C). Option 2 is not available to foreign manufacturers.
- The applicant first registers on the BIS IT system and gets a unique code, submits product samples to a third party laboratory with that code, and uploads the laboratory receipt.
- The application is submitted with conforming test report(s) of the product (and of raw materials, if applicable) issued by a third party laboratory.
- Accepted test reports: laboratories established, maintained or recognised by BIS for the product (including Group-2 labs under the Laboratory Recognition Scheme), and government laboratories empanelled by BIS.
- Test reports must not be more than 90 days old, counted from the date of issue to the date the application is received. With multiple reports, the oldest must not be more than 180 days old.
- BIS still visits the factory to assess it and draws samples for testing in a third party laboratory.

## Factory visit
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GrantofLicence-Guidelines-25Feb2026.pdf -->
The factory visit normally lasts one day for Indian manufacturers and two days for foreign manufacturers. During the visit BIS verifies the self-evaluation cum verification report, details of quality control personnel, calibration certificates of testing equipment, test certificates of raw material, the plant layout, and draws samples of the product or components as applicable.

## Fees and timelines
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-faq/ -->
- Application fee: Rs. 1,000.
- Inspection fee: Rs. 7,000 per man-day, paid before the preliminary inspection visit.
- After BIS decides to grant the licence: annual licence fee of Rs. 1,000 plus the minimum marking fee specified for the product (Annexure I of Scheme I of the BIS Conformity Assessment Regulations, 2018, as amended).
- Average time to grant a licence: about one month under Option 2 and about four months under Option 1, from receipt of a complete application. Delays in replies, inspections, samples or fees can extend this.
- A Scheme-I licence may be granted initially for up to two years and renewed for up to five years at a time through the Manakonline dashboard.

## After the licence is granted
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-faq/ -->
BIS carries out surprise factory surveillance inspections and draws samples of ISI marked products from the factory and the market for third party testing. The licence covers only the varieties listed in its scope; the scope can be changed under the Guidelines for Change in Scope of Licence. The licence can be suspended or cancelled under the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 if its conditions are violated. The licence number is shown with the Standard Mark as CM/L-XXXXXXXXXX.
