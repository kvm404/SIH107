# How to find the Indian Standard and certification rules for a product

Hand-curated from BIS web pages. Wording follows the official pages.

## Finding the Indian Standard for your product
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-faq/ -->
A manufacturer who does not know the Indian Standard for a product can search for it on Manakonline at https://www.manakonline.in/MANAK/ApplicationLicenceRelatedrpt. If the standard cannot be found, send a query to the concerned technical department of BIS at https://www.bis.gov.in/php/BIS/biswebsite/standards/technical-departments/.

## Know Your Standard
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/know-your-standard/ -->
The Know Your Standard feature gives one-stop access to the documents and data for a standard. Search by IS number or by keyword such as a product name. It shows the standard, amendments, gazette notifications, the scheme of testing and inspection, the list of licences, the laboratories that test for the standard, classification details and committee composition. The same search is available in the BIS Care app under Know your Standards.

## Is certification compulsory for my product?
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/ -->
BIS certification is basically voluntary. For some products the Central Government makes conformity to Indian Standards compulsory through Quality Control Orders (QCOs), for reasons such as public interest, health, safety, environment, prevention of unfair trade practices and national security. For those products the Standard Mark must be used under a BIS licence or Certificate of Conformity. If your product is on the list of products under compulsory certification, a BIS licence is compulsory for it. The lists are: Scheme-I (ISI Mark), Scheme-II (Compulsory Registration Scheme), Scheme-IV (Certificate of Conformity) and upcoming QCOs that are notified but not yet in force.

## Free Indian Standards
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/ -->
BIS offers indigenous Indian Standards as a free download from its compulsory certification page ("Indigenous Indian Standards – Free Download").

## Technical guidance for a product
<!-- source: https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/product-certification-faq/ -->
Product Manuals are available for products under the Product Certification Scheme. A product manual includes sampling guidelines, the list of test equipment, the Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT) and a description of scope. They are on the BIS website under Conformity Assessment, Product Certification, Product Specific Guidelines, Product Manuals.
